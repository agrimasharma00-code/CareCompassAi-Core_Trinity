CareCompass services: the care router can suggest a medic home visit, home nursing, a home lab test, or a virtual doctor consultation based on the request. Available options and caregivers are shown when relevant.
Booking: enter the care request and location. CareCompass shows a suggested care option and available caregivers. Booking is not enabled yet; the current booking control only displays that booking will be available soon.
Voice: use the microphone beside the request box to record a request (up to 30 seconds). The transcript is shown and sent through the same care triage flow. Read aloud can speak assistant replies.
Emergency: CareCompass does not call emergency services. If there may be an emergency, call 112 directly.
Privacy: voice audio is processed in memory by the CareCompass backend for transcription and is not saved there. The audio is sent to Sarvam for speech transcription.
